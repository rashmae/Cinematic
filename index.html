Frequently Asked Questions

How does SmartSpace prevent double bookings across university venues?

SmartSpace employs a real-time reservation conflict engine. The moment a timeslot is reserved or approved by a university facility administrator, that specific room and hour block is locked against conflicting submissions across all departments, student organizations, and academic colleges.

Can student organizations book facilities across partner universities?

Yes. SmartSpace supports cross-university facility reservations. Each participating institution defines its own booking clearance policy (e.g. host college dean endorsement or accredited student council clearance). Partner campus organizations can request spaces transparently.

How are physical campus gates and keys managed on event day?

Once an institutional administrator approves a reservation, SmartSpace generates a time-bounded, encrypted QR Gate Pass. Campus security and building custodians scan this pass with any mobile device to immediately confirm authorized venue occupancy.

What equipment and operating constraints can administrators set?

Institutional administrators can customize advance booking windows (e.g. 48h to 5 days), required endorsement attachments (such as adviser signatures or physical therapist coverage for gym events), maximum capacities, and operating schedules per space.

How can a university or college system onboard with SmartSpace?

Higher education institutions can onboard in under 48 hours. Simply request a demo or submit an institutional registration application. Our deployment team provisions your dedicated campus facility sandbox with custom approval hierarchies.
